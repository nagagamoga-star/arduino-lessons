The simulator runs a beginner-sized subset of Arduino C++ (variables, functions, if, for, while, arrays, Serial, Servo, Stepper and LiquidCrystal) by translating it to JavaScript. Real parts are noisier, and timing is approximate, so check each part's datasheet before you wire the real thing. Your code and progress stay in this browser.
